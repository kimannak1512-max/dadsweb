Tools & Materials RequiredHave all of this ready before dismantling starts
  • Metric or imperial socket/spanner set matching the pump manufacturer (Grasso, Mycom/Mayekawa, Frick, Vilter, Sabroe, GEA, etc.)
  • Torque wrench
  • Bearing puller
  • Feeler gauges (for backlash / clearance checks)
  • New gaskets, O-rings, and shaft seal kit — never reuse elastomer seals on ammonia service
  • Ammonia-compatible thread sealant / gasket compound
  • Clean lint-free rags and a parts cleaner compatible with ammonia oil (avoid chlorinated solvents)
  • Manufacturer's exploded parts diagram and torque spec sheet
  • Drip trays for oil capture

Inspection ChecklistCheck every item below before reassembly begins
  • Gear teeth for pitting, scoring, or wear steps
  • Shaft for scoring at the seal contact area
  • Bearing races for wear, discoloration (overheating), or play
  • Casing bore for scoring from gear-to-housing contact
  • Relief valve for spring fatigue and seat wear
  • Gasket faces for flatness — check with a straightedge
  • Gear backlash and end-clearance measured against the manufacturer's spec sheet
